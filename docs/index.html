Frequently asked questions

 

How do I completely uninstall an app on Mac?

Dragging an app to the Trash leaves its caches, preferences, containers and logs behind in ~/Library. Open AppSweep, select the app, review the related files it found and click Uninstall — the app and all its files move to the Trash together.

Is AppSweep free?

Yes — completely free and open source under the MIT license. No subscriptions, ads or in-app purchases.

Is AppSweep a free alternative to CleanMyMac or AppCleaner?

AppSweep covers the most common uses of those tools — uninstalling apps with their leftovers and cleaning caches — for free, and its source code is public so anyone can see exactly what it does.

How do I clear cache on a Mac?

Open AppSweep → Cache Cleaner, select the app caches, logs or developer caches (like Xcode DerivedData) you want to remove, and click Clean. Apps rebuild their caches automatically when needed.

Is it safe?

Yes. Everything goes to the Trash by default so it can be restored, running apps are quit first, and macOS system apps are protected. AppSweep works fully offline and collects no data.

macOS says “Apple could not verify AppSweep”. What do I do?

AppSweep isn't notarized by Apple yet. Use the one-line installer above, or after installing the DMG run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/AppSweep.app once — or click Open Anyway in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Does it work on Apple Silicon (M1–M4) and Intel?

Yes. AppSweep is a universal app for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs running macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
